    How to Play

    Guess the mystery Rainbow Six Siege operator in 10 tries. After every guess, each attribute is scored against the mystery operator:

    • Exact match
    • No match
    • Mystery operator's value is higher
    • Mystery operator's value is lower

    Extra hints — gadgets, secondary weapon, and primary weapon — unlock automatically as you use up guesses. Classic mode is unlimited random rounds; Daily Challenge gives everyone the same operator once per day and tracks your streak.
